\subsection{What is the probability that after X days the order will be an aged order?}\label{subsec:survival-analysis}

We look at the chance of an order becoming an aged order in this
section. Here, we build a survival curve. Survival means that an order
has yet to encounter a accepted document upload or a cancellation. The
The cancellations after day 10 are system created aged order
cancellations. From figure \ref{fig:first-order-line-survival}, that
after day 3, the change of an order becoming an aged order is about
~50\% (28.90\%/55.80\%) if there is no accepted document upload or any
cancellation except aged order.

Figure~\ref{fig:first-order-line-survival} shows the probability that a first-order
line is still awaiting an accepted upload or cancellation after order placement.
