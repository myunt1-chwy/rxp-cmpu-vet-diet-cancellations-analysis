\subsubsection{Section Reproduction Steps}\label{subsubsec:reproduce-upload-delays}
After \texttt{make setup}, ensure Docker is running and accessible for the
Mermaid Sankey renderer. From the repository root, run:
\begin{lstlisting}[basicstyle=\ttfamily\small,breaklines=true]
make reproduce-upload-delay-section
make report
\end{lstlisting}
This replays the retained August 15 inclusive--October 15 exclusive prescription
cohort, August 1--October 25 upload discovery window, and October 27, 2026 aged
cancellation cutoff. The section target runs:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \path{study-population-tables}: validates and renders
Tables~\ref{tab:study-population-counts} and
\ref{tab:study-population-prescription-decisions}, then invokes
\path{prescription-cohort-order-comparison} for
Table~\ref{tab:first-all-order-cancellations}.
\item \path{cmpu-first-order-line-status}: regenerates the
\texttt{CUSTOMER\_UPLOAD} Sankey in Figure~\ref{fig:first-order-lines-customer-upload}.
\item \path{cmpu-upload-entity-order-creation-distributions}: regenerates
Figure~\ref{fig:entity-upload-vs-order-creation}.
\item \path{cmpu-upload-entity-state-change-distributions}: regenerates
Figures~\ref{fig:entity-upload-state-time-overall} and
\ref{fig:entity-upload-state-time-aged}.
\item \path{cmpu-upload-to-approval-distribution}: regenerates
Figure~\ref{fig:accepted-upload-to-approval-cumulative}.
\end{itemize}
All inputs are fingerprint-checked retained snapshots under \path{data/}; no
warehouse refresh occurs. The two population tables retain their frozen
first-order membership, while the financial comparison uses its documented
later snapshot. Exact replay requires these ignored extracts and manifests.
See HOWTO.md for source SQL and extraction steps; new snapshots can change
membership and must be reconciled before replacing the frozen populations.
