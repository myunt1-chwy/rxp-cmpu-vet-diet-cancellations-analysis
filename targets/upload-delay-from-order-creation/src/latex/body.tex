Upload creation relative to order placement for the overall
and aged-order entity populations. Elapsed time is upload creation minus order
placement: positive values indicate upload after ordering and negative values
indicate upload before ordering. Each entity uses its earliest matched first-order
placement. Plotted durations include 29,715 overall entities and 782 aged-order
entities; entities missing an order-placement timestamp are excluded from the
boxplots. Medians, quartiles, 1.5-times-IQR whiskers and all outliers are shown.
