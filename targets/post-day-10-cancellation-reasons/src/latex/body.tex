Table~\ref{tab:post-day-10-cancellation-reasons} lists the recorded
reasons for first-order lines cancelled more than 10 days after order
placement. Pharmacists record \emph{Prescription Issue} when no

order cancellation. The cohort uses the same prescription window as
Figure~\ref{fig:first-order-line-survival}, but is drawn directly from
