\label{subsec:medium-term-reorder-behavior}
\noindent\textbf{Cohorts.}
A qualifying aged order contains a
vet-diet line linked to a vet-diet prescription with source CMPU and approval
channel CUSTOMER\_UPLOAD, cancelled for the reason ``aged order'' from
May 1 through July 31, 2026, inclusive, UTC. The original aged line need not
have shipped and may have any autoship status. Prescription creation dates
do not restrict this population.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{All customers with an aged order (All).} Include each customer
with at least one qualifying aged order, regardless of earlier purchases.
Every qualifying aged line provides a cancellation date from which to
measure reorders; a customer may therefore have several anchors.

\item \textbf{Aged order is the first CMPU CUSTOMER\_UPLOAD vet-diet
order.} Find each customer's earliest placed vet-diet order linked to a
CMPU prescription with approval channel CUSTOMER\_UPLOAD, across all
available history. Include the customer only if that very order qualifies
as aged in the study period. Earlier orders from another prescription
source or approval channel do not exclude the customer; an earlier CMPU
CUSTOMER\_UPLOAD vet-diet order does, even if it was cancelled or never
shipped. Only the qualifying aged lines on that first order provide
follow-up anchors.

\item \textbf{Aged order is the first-ever vet-diet order.} Find each
customer's earliest placed vet-diet order across all prescription sources
and approval channels. Include the customer only if that very order
qualifies as aged in the study period. Any earlier vet-diet order excludes
the customer, even if cancelled or unshipped; earlier purchases of other
products do not. Only the qualifying aged lines on that first order
provide follow-up anchors.

\item \textbf{Aged order is the first Chewy order.} Find each customer's
earliest placed Chewy order in available order history, including all
products and statuses. Include the customer only if that very order
qualifies as aged in the study period. Any earlier Chewy order excludes
the customer, even if cancelled or unshipped. Only the qualifying aged
lines on that first order provide follow-up anchors.
\end{enumerate}

First-order classification uses available historical records and currently
recorded prescription attributes, including all order statuses. Customers
whose first order cannot be established because relevant order records or
placement times are missing, or because distinct earliest orders tie, are
excluded from the first-order cohorts. Later aged orders do not reset their
follow-up. Each percentage uses its cohort's fixed distinct-customer count,
including customers without a qualifying reorder or with unusable matching
keys. Later-order eligibility affects the numerator, not cohort membership.
The cohorts overlap; do not add their counts.

\par\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Types of reorders discussed.}
Each cohort's six tables use the definitions below. A reorder is a later
qualifying order placed by the same customer after an aged-order cancellation.
Only matching shipped line items count. Products are matched by part number;
autoship classification uses the line item, not the order-level flag. All
six types follow the successful-order and follow-up rules below.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Same-pet, same-product reorders.} The later line item matches
both the pet and the part number on an aged line item. This includes
non-autoship lines, first autoship shipments and recurring autoship shipments.

\item \textbf{Non-autoship reorders.} The later line item matches the same
pet and part number and is classified as non-autoship. Lines absent from the
subscription transaction records are treated as non-autoship; lines with
an unknown autoship flag in an existing record do not qualify.

\item \textbf{Reorders where line item is the first line item in an
autoship subscription.}
The later line item matches the same pet and part number, is an autoship
line, and belongs to the earliest recorded shipment of that product within
its subscription. All line items tied at that earliest shipment time qualify.
Subsequent shipments of the same product within that subscription do not
qualify for this table. Missing subscription or product identifiers, or a
missing shipment time, cannot establish a first shipment.

\item \textbf{Same-pet, different-product reorders.} The later vet-diet line
item matches the pet on an aged line item but has a different part number.
This captures a change in product for the same pet, with no autoship restriction.

\item \textbf{Any vet-diet reorders.} The later line item is a vet-diet
product for any pet and any part number, with no autoship restriction.

\item \textbf{Chewy-wide reorders.} The later line item may be in any product
category, for any pet and any part number, with no autoship restriction.
\end{enumerate}

The non-autoship and first-autoship tables are subsets of same-pet,
same-product reorders. Customers and orders can appear in several tables,
and an order may contain multiple line types. Do not sum counts or sales
across these overlapping views.

\par\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Shared methodology.}
These rules apply to the reorder comparisons, not the cancellation baseline.

\textit{Matching and follow-up.} Require the same numeric customer ID and a
 different order placed strictly after the matched cancellation through day
N inclusive. Windows run from 7 through 70 days and require complete follow-up
through 2026-10-28 00:00 UTC. Pet and part-number restrictions appear with each
comparison; when required, pet IDs must be non-null and part numbers nonblank.
Later orders may use any approval channel or prescription source.
The matching line item must have shipped.
Shipment presence, statuses and channels reflect currently recorded data;
windows and cutoff apply to order placement, not shipment dates.

\textit{Successful orders.} Exclude the entire later order if any line has status \texttt{X}, a nonblank
cancellation reason, a cancellation timestamp or NULL line status. The
Chewy-wide comparison also excludes an order-header status of \texttt{X}.

\textit{Counts and sales.} Each customer, order and matching physical line
counts once per window, despite repeated anchor associations and shipments.
Cumulative windows overlap; do not add counts. Net sales sum
\texttt{ORDER\_LINE\_NET\_SALES} once per matching shipment/line contribution,
preserving zero and negative amounts. Sales can be recorded after the
placement window and do not prove recovered revenue. An asterisk or
\textit{Incomplete} marks missing sales; N/A denotes a zero denominator.
Amounts display two decimals.
