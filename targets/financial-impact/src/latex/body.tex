Table~\ref{tab:financial-opportunity-cancellations} reports distinct customer and order counts,

\par\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Shared methodology for the reorder comparisons below.}
These rules apply to the reorder comparisons, not the cancellation baseline.

\textit{Cohorts and anchors.} All aged customers includes every qualifying
aged cancellation in the study period, without a prescription-creation or
first-order restriction. The first CUSTOMER\_UPLOAD cohort requires the
customer's earliest observed vet-diet CUSTOMER\_UPLOAD order to have aged;
the first vet-diet cohort requires the earliest observed vet-diet order,
across approval channels and prescription sources, to have aged. Historical
RxP lines, linked prescriptions and ECOM order history establish first orders.
For these first-order cohorts that order is the sole anchor; later aged orders
do not reset follow-up. Percentages use each fixed full cohort, including
customers without a qualifying reorder or with unusable matching keys.
Cohorts overlap; do not add their counts.

\textit{Matching and follow-up.} Require the same numeric customer ID and a
 different order placed strictly after the matched cancellation through day
N inclusive. Windows run from 7 through 70 days and require complete follow-up
through 2026-10-28 00:00 UTC. Pet and part-number restrictions appear with each
comparison; when required, pet IDs must be non-null and part numbers nonblank.
Later orders may use any approval channel or prescription source.
Each matching physical line must satisfy
\texttt{MIN(ORDER\_LINE\_SHIPPED\_DTTM) IS NOT NULL}, grouped by
\texttt{(order\_id, order\_line\_id)}; shipment of a sibling line does not qualify it.
Shipment presence, statuses and channels reflect currently recorded data;
windows and cutoff apply to order placement, not shipment dates.

\textit{Successful orders.} Exclude the entire later order if any line has status \texttt{X}, a nonblank
cancellation reason, a cancellation timestamp or NULL line status. The
Chewy-wide comparison also excludes an order-header status of \texttt{X}.
Autoship comparisons use line-level transaction flags; the order autoreorder
flag is ignored. A missing subscription transaction row means non-autoship;
a NULL flag on a present row remains unknown. Initial autoship shipments
require an autoship line at the earliest recorded shipment time for that
product within its subscription, considering all available shipment history.
Lines tied at that earliest timestamp all qualify. Missing subscription or
product identifiers, or a missing shipment time, cannot qualify.

\textit{Counts and sales.} Each customer, order and matching physical line
counts once per window, despite repeated anchor associations and shipments.
Cumulative windows overlap; do not add counts. Net sales sum
\texttt{ORDER\_LINE\_NET\_SALES} once per matching shipment/line contribution,
preserving zero and negative amounts. Sales can be recorded after the
placement window and do not prove recovered revenue. An asterisk or
\textit{Incomplete} marks missing sales; N/A denotes a zero denominator.
Amounts display two decimals.
