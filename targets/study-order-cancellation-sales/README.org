* Cohort definition
SQL derives the prescription creation cohort, converts numeric first-order
item IDs to line keys, and selects both first-order keys and all linked vet-diet
lines matching prescription and customer. Physical order/line keys are
unioned and deduplicated before shipment evidence is selected. Sales retain
the shipment-level source grain; shipment aggregation and distinct physical
line/order counts are validated before presentation.

The query is self-contained: inputs are scalar parameters, not saved row or
identifier lists. Review its target-owned =src/sql/= source;
=make cohort-sql-review= renders the registered cohort queries and
=make cohort-sql-check= lints them without warehouse access.
