* Cohort definition
The contact queries derive CMPU vet-diet CUSTOMER_UPLOAD first-order
prescriptions in the creation window and aged cancellations with mature
7/14/21-day follow-up windows. Reorders must match customer, product ID and an
unambiguous pet. Select the shortest-delay reorder per customer and horizon;
break ties by reorder timestamp, reorder order/line IDs and original order/line
IDs. Deduplicate intervals and assign deterministic order/time sequence IDs.
The WISMO query restricts contacts to handled WISMO records. The companion
all-contact and all-order-history queries retain their own handled-contact and
interval/history filters. Dates and contact timezone are scalar parameters.

The query is self-contained: inputs are scalar parameters, not saved row or
identifier lists. Review its target-owned =src/sql/= source;
=make cohort-sql-review= renders the registered cohort queries and
=make cohort-sql-check= lints them without warehouse access.
