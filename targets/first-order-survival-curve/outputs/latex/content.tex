\paragraph{What proportion of first-order lines remain without an accepted upload or cancellation over time?}
Kaplan--Meier survival curve for 94,869 eligible distinct
first-order lines linked to CMPU vet-diet \texttt{CUSTOMER\_UPLOAD} prescriptions
created August 15--October 14, 2026, inclusive, UTC. Time zero is order placement;
the event is the earlier of the actual or estimated accepted-entity state-change
time and order cancellation. The upload-entity discovery window is August
1--October 25, 2026, inclusive. Missing events and events after October 26, 2026
at 00:00 UTC are right-censored at that cutoff; events exactly at the cutoff
are observed. Survival means neither accepted-state change nor
cancellation has occurred.
